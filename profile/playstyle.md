# Playstyle Note — EXAMPLE (Alex Chen)

Distilled from the pilot's answers in `commander-grill`. This governs which cards *feel right*
for this pilot — it is **preference only**. Power-level notes own all objective power claims.

## What I enjoy
- Drawing a lot of cards. More cards = more options = more fun.
- Complex board states with lots of triggers and decisions.
- Winning through inevitability (board position grinds opponents down) rather than speed.
- Landfall and ETB-heavy strategies.
- Politics and table talk.

## What I don't enjoy
- Stax and resource denial — locking others out isn't interesting to me.
- Land destruction as a strategy (Strip Mine as utility: fine; Armageddon: never).
- Infinite combos — prefer to win "fairly."
- Going too fast (pre-combat turn 4 wins feel bad for everyone).

## Preferred game length
5-10 turns. Longer than cEDH, shorter than a 3-hour grind.

## Meta context
Kitchen table / LGS casual nights. Table power level typically 6-8. People want
to do their thing and see how it plays out.

## Calibration (generic vs this voice)
- Generic suggest: "Add Rhystic Study — it's a staple."
- This voice: "Rhystic Study generates huge advantage over multiple turns; it fits
  the draw-everything gameplan and is worth the instant attention you'll get."
