# Aesi, Tyrant of Gyre Strait — Landfall Value (EXAMPLE)

An example deck record showing the shape the `brew` skill produces. Replace with your own.

## What the deck does

Draw enormous amounts of cards through Aesi's triggered ability by playing as many lands
per turn as possible. Ramp into an overwhelming board position with token generators
(Avenger of Zendikar, Scute Swarm, Rampaging Baloths), then close through combat with
Craterhoof Behemoth, or assemble Scapeshift + Field of the Dead for a zombie army.

## Constraints

- Power level target: Focused 7 (per power-level-notes.md)
- Budget: ~$400 paper (several proxies in the land base)
- No infinite combos (power-level-notes.md hard rule)
- No stax

## Why Aesi

Aesi is the engine, not a threat — he draws cards until opponents answer him, then the
deck has been drawing cards the whole time anyway. Every game feels different because the
order of extra-land-drop effects matters and players choose their lines.
